## Hook (10s bucket) — canonical prompt

```

Quality / Fidelity Lock: Use the exact same lighting, texture, and iPhone image quality as @image1. Do not sharpen, smooth skin, or apply any beauty filter, HDR, color grade, or cinematic polish. Keep it raw and true to @image1 — warm natural daylight, sunlit green foliage, realistic exposure, visible freckles and natural skin texture.

Reference / Identity: Use @image1 as the first frame and the exact scene, background, and framing. Use @charecterset as the identity reference — preserve her fair freckled skin, green/hazel eyes, auburn wavy shoulder-length hair, real facial structure and age. Use @lymphoriabottle as the product — match the green-and-cream "LYMPHORIA lymphatic drainage" label, shape, and colors exactly; the label must never warp, morph, or distort. Keep her in the same white off-the-shoulder eyelet top as @image1.

Scene: Keep the exact same background as @image1 — a dense, sunlit green leafy hedge directly behind her. Do not recreate or change the background; keep it identical and stable. Do not add people, roads, or pavement.

Camera: Handheld iPhone selfie held in her own hand — constant natural shake (small sway, drift, tiny bounces) the whole time; never tripod-static or locked-off. No zoom, pan, whip-pan, or cuts. Medium close-up, eye-level, matched to @image1, with the bottle on the same side of the frame as @image1.

Performance & Micro-Movement: She talks fast and candid like a real TikTok creator — animated, never stiff. Throughout: natural uneven blinking, continuous subtle breathing, small weight shifts, live head movement, constant facial micro-expressions. She holds the bottle in one hand the whole time with a relaxed, freely-moving wrist (never rigid), label toward camera; her other hand gestures. On "Do not buy," a mock-serious warning look, brows slightly knit, direct eye contact, small head dip, and she lifts the bottle on "Lymphoria." On "they said it would help," brows lift into relatable venting, free hand gestures down toward her legs, quick head tilt and brow-flash on "okay?" On "how fast it would work," brows raise high with a slight head pull-back, eyes widen, fewer blinks, a small amused smirk creeping in.

Voice: Warm, natural, everyday-woman American voice with a light, casual Southern lilt — real and slightly textured, never polished or announcer-like. Fast-paced TikTok-style delivery: energetic, quick, and run-on, like she's excitedly rattling off her story to her followers, not calm or composed. She talks fast and casual with natural momentum and candid emphasis, while still staying clear and easy to understand.

Dialogue: She speaks directly to camera with accurate lip sync at a fast, natural, conversational TikTok pace, saying exactly: "Do not buy Lymphoria lymphatic drainage for cellulite. Audio / Environment Sound: Realistic iPhone-recorded outdoor audio — soft natural ambience, light breeze, faint distant birds, a gentle rustle of leaves. Voice close and real to the phone mic, not studio-polished. Avoid traffic, indoor room tone, crowd noise, music, or cinematic sound design.

Style: Authentic TikTok/Reels UGC. Raw handheld smartphone selfie footage with natural camera shake. Natural skin texture with visible freckles. Slightly imperfect, candid realism.

Negative Constraints: No captions, no on-screen text, no logos other than the bottle label, no morphing, no warping, no extra or missing fingers. Do not mirror, flip, or horizontally reverse the shot — keep the same left-right layout as @image1, including the bare shoulder on the same side. Do not recreate or change the background. Do not make the camera tripod-static — natural handheld shake must stay visible. Do not change her outfit, hair, skin, or the bottle.
```

**Duration:** 10 seconds.
**Assets to use, in order:** 1) `@image1` (scene + first frame + wardrobe) · 2) `@charecterset` (identity) · 3) `@lymphoriabottle` (product label). No external audio.
