# Location master: Pixar high fidelity

> IMMUTABLE. Copy everything inside the block unchanged, then append two line breaks and the complete location description (see references/locations.md).

```text
Create ONE HIGH-FIDELITY STYLIZED 3D LOCATION REFERENCE IMAGE.

STYLE

Use polished cinematic 3D rendering with rich materials, subtly sculpted forms, restrained stylization, and gently softened edges.

Keep a visible designed 3D quality. Avoid strong Pixar-like furniture exaggeration, oversized props, toy-like construction, and full photographic realism.

Show material depth through fabric weave, wood grain, ceramic roughness, tile variation, brushed metal, and subtle wear, as appropriate to the location.

Use rich global illumination, nuanced contact shadows, restrained reflections, and excellent anti-aliasing. Avoid uniformly glossy surfaces and plastic-looking furniture.

COMPOSITION

Create one wide image, not a collage or a multi-panel sheet.

For an interior, use a natural eye-level ¾ angled view showing room depth and two adjoining walls. For an exterior, use a ¾ angled view revealing the front and side of the building and its surroundings.

Establish a clear foreground, middle ground, and background. Keep architecture coherent, furniture placement practical, and walking routes usable.

Show the layout clearly, with broad enough depth of field to read the environment. Avoid an overhead view, flat frontal composition, or extreme wide-angle distortion.

PERSONALITY, COMPOSITION, AND BUDGET

Follow the complete location description below for the resident or workplace, named design style, architecture, palette, furniture, wall treatments, and exact placement of personal objects.

Default to ordinary lower- or middle-income settings unless the description explicitly establishes wealth. Render quality must not turn a modest setting into a luxury property.

Use purposeful decoration and believable belongings accumulated over time. Show appropriate curtains, shelving, artwork, textiles, practical storage, and signs of daily use.

Allow small imperfections in arrangement, subtle wear, and furniture that coordinates without matching perfectly. Keep the space functional and reasonably clean.

Avoid generic empty walls, showroom-perfect staging, and clutter that blocks the room. Keep drawers and cabinets closed unless the described scene provides a reason to open them. Use a few natural signs of use in a reasonably tidy setting; do not create a complete mess.

LIGHTING — DRAMATIC AND BEAUTIFUL

Use expressive, motivated cinematic lighting appropriate to the story, time of day, windows, and practical fixtures.

Create a clear directional light source, luminous highlights, deeper shaded areas, rich bounced light, dimensional shadows, and smooth highlight roll-off.

Balance warm and cool light where appropriate. Keep shaded areas readable.

Use restrained cinematic bloom around the brightest window edges, illuminated curtain edges, or practical lights. Allow a soft luminous halo without washing out textures or obscuring the room.

Subtle atmospheric haze may reveal a motivated shaft of light where appropriate. Avoid heavy fog, giant halos, crushed blacks, and blown highlights.

COLOR GRADING

Use a rich cohesive palette drawn from the location description, selective saturation, nuanced material colors, and sophisticated cinematic contrast.

Avoid neon saturation, aggressive orange grading, and artificial HDR.

ABSOLUTE CONSTRAINTS

No people.
No animals.
No human or animal figures in artwork, photographs, screens, or reflections.
No readable text, labels, logos, watermarks, or layout graphics.
No extravagant architecture unless explicitly requested.
```
