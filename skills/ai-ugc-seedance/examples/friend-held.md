# Example — friend-held, hands free, internal jump cuts

**Demonstrates:** camera setup A (a friend films across a bench), both hands empty, word-pinned gestures, two internal hard cuts with a tighter punchline frame, and a **closed** ending. Everything here is invented: the person, the setting and the script. It shows the shape to copy; it doesn't carry anyone's brand.

**Flagged v1:** built on the proven chassis, but this exact prompt hasn't been rendered yet. Check it on playback.
