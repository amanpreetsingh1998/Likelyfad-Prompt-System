# Delivery — clip series (one ad across several generations)

Production ads were built as **series of 5–12s clips** stitched in the edit. This is how they keep one continuous performance. Tags: **[proven]** · **[guide]** · **[trial]**.

## Plan the series before writing any clip [proven]
1. **Split the script by breath and meaning,** not by fixed length: one clip = one thought, or 2–5 short beats of one thought.
2. **Decide each clip's ending:**
   - **Open (mid-thought):** the next clip continues the same sentence. The face is held with a small "and…" anticipation, lips slightly parted, a soft inhale, and no settled pose.
   - **Closed:** the thought lands. The expression is settled and the exhale is audible.
3. **Map the register** across the series, and mark where it genuinely shifts (anger → trusted recommendation). Everywhere else it carries forward unchanged.
4. **Deliver the plan first:** clip number, duration, lines, open or closed.

## Continuity between clips [proven]
- **Same wording, every clip:** the wardrobe description, the background block, the look block and the camera line are pasted word for word.
- **Start where the last clip ended:** open the next clip's subject block in that state, e.g. `lips already parted with the post-inhale anticipation from the previous beat, eyebrows already in the scolding state`.
- **Name the register's origin:** `continuing directly from the previous clip's "<last word>" close, no register reset`.
- **First frame per clip:** a still for each clip keeps composition exact. When a clip needs the previous clip's exact last frame, extend from video instead: `@video1 as the video to extend from — continue from the exact last frame.`
- **Hard cuts between clips** don't need the previous clip's last frame. Identity and background references plus identical wording are enough [guide].

## Hard cuts inside one clip [proven]
- Up to **4 internal hard cuts** in a 10s clip rendered well.
- The camera line states the count: `with three internal hard cuts`.
- Each cut is its own bracketed line:
  ```
  [HARD CUT — instant frame-to-frame transition at 2.5s, slight 5-10 degree angle pivot, same composition scale, NO zoom, NO scale change, NO motion blur, NO dissolve, NO fade]
  ```
- **A tighter frame on a punchline** is a deliberate exception: `5-20 percent tighter framing, head and upper chest fill more of the frame` or `10-15% tighter framing on <character>`. Say how much tighter.
- **A scene change inside one clip** (another pose or another spot in the same room) gets its own held-position reference image, `matching @image3`.
- **Prop continuity across cuts:** state the prop's state in each cut's line, e.g. `Bottle still in his right hand, towel still around his neck`, or `The towel is no longer visible in this cut`.
- **Cuts are silent**, and the voice carries across them.

## Series audit
- Every open clip's last word has comma cadence and an inhale; the next clip starts mid-sentence (`...and`).
- The wardrobe, background, look and camera lines are identical across the series.
- Register shifts happen only where the plan says.
- A prop that appears in clip 4 isn't silently present in clip 3.
